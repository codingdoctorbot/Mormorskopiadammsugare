**Move instead of copy** – empty your backups of photos and videos once each one is safely in the new
collection, with an undo button.

**Download `Mormorskopiadammsugare.exe`** below (Windows 10/11, 64-bit, self-contained – no install).

### What's new
- **Move instead of copy** (off every time the app starts, confirmed before every run): unique photos and
  videos are moved (instant on the same drive; copied and checked byte by byte on another drive), duplicates
  are deleted – each only after the kept copy in the destination has been checked.
- **Never removed, only copied:** program and game folders (also folders next to `.dll` files), read-only
  files, synced cloud folders (OneDrive, Google Drive) and suspect files. The log says why (`KeptInSource`).
- Already copied everything with v0.1.0? Run again with move ticked and the **same destination**: nothing is
  copied again, the originals are checked against their copies and removed.
- **Restore originals…** puts every moved or deleted file back at its old place, name and date, from the
  destination (checked against the original). The destination is not changed.
- Catalog upgrades automatically (schema v3).

**Afterwards each photo exists once – back up the destination to a second drive.** Without move mode,
nothing changes: your original files are never changed, moved or deleted.

### Verify the download
SmartScreen may warn because the exe isn't code-signed. It was built by GitHub Actions from this
tag; check the signed build attestation and/or the checksum:

```powershell
gh attestation verify Mormorskopiadammsugare.exe --repo codingdoctorbot/Mormorskopiadammsugare
Get-FileHash Mormorskopiadammsugare.exe   # compare with SHA256SUMS.txt
```

### Known limitations
Move mode leaves the emptied folders behind. Exact duplicates only (resized/re-compressed copies are kept);
MTS/MKV/WMV/MPG videos are dated by names only; ZIP archives aren't opened.
